2. What the app shows

For each upcoming football match, the app looks for the 10 finished matches whose pre-match statistics were the most similar, and shows what happened in them: final and half-time scores, goals and goal minutes, and match statistics such as shots, corners and cards.

  • All scores and statistics in the app belong to past, already finished matches. They are not the result of the upcoming match.
  • Similar matches are selected automatically by a statistical comparison. "Similar" means similar pre-match numbers; it does not mean the matches are the same, or that the upcoming match will go the same way.
  • Similar matches can come from other leagues, seasons and years, and the teams' own past matches can appear too.
  • A match's similar matches are set once, when the match joins the list, and are not updated afterwards.
  • Summaries on the match screen (results, goals, averages) are simple counts and averages of those 10 past matches.

3. Not a prediction, no guarantee

Nothing in the app is a prediction, forecast, tip, recommendation or guarantee about the result of any match. What happened in similar past matches does not mean the same will happen in the upcoming match. Football results are uncertain, and any match can end in any way.

Statistic names used in the app (such as "BTTS" or "Over 2.5") only describe what happened in past matches.

Any decision you make using information from the app, including any bet, is made entirely at your own risk and responsibility. If you choose to gamble, do so only where it is legal for you, only if you are of legal age, and only with money you can afford to lose.

5. Accuracy of the data

Match data is compiled automatically from third-party sources and is published as it is. We work to keep it correct, but we do not guarantee that it is accurate, complete or up to date. In particular:

  • scores, goal minutes and statistics may be missing, delayed or wrong for some matches;
  • some statistics (for example goal minutes or half-time scores) are not available for every match;
  • kick-off times may change and matches may be postponed or cancelled after they appear in the list;
  • there are no live scores: "Started" and "Ended" are based only on the kick-off time;
  • the list is refreshed a few times a day, so new or changed matches may appear late.
